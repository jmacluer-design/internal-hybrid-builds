Controller check

Confirm your Xbox / PlayStation pad and VR controllers connect before you trust a game with them. Nothing here is sent anywhere.
← back to the shelf

1. Gamepads (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, 8BitDo...)

No controller detected yet. Press any button on your pad (browsers hide pads until you do).
Pair it first (the browser can only see a pad the device has already paired):
  • Xbox: hold the round sync button on top until the logo flashes fast, then pair in the device's Bluetooth settings.
  • PlayStation 4/5: hold Share + PS until the light bar flashes, then pair in Bluetooth settings.
  • iPad / iPhone / Apple TV / Android / Fire TV / Windows / Mac: Bluetooth settings → pair. USB cable works on computers too.
  • Quest: pair a Bluetooth pad in the headset's settings (menu names vary by Horizon OS version), then open this page in Quest Browser.
Default game mapping (all games on the shelf): left stick = move, right stick = look, A = jump, RT = primary action, LT = secondary / hold, X = J, B = Z, Y = E, LB / R3 = Q, RB / L3 = Shift, Start = confirm / pause.

2. VR (Meta Quest Touch controllers, or a PC headset through Chrome)

In the headset you get a floating panel that lists every controller it can see, with live button and stick values. Squeeze a trigger to feel a haptic pulse. VR needs HTTPS (this site is) and a VR-capable browser: Quest Browser on a Quest, or Chrome / Edge on a PC with Link / SteamVR / Windows Mixed Reality. Safari and Firefox on phones/tablets do not do WebXR VR.
