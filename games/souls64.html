A ONE-ROOM BOSS RITUAL

SOULS64

One arena. One boss. A hero who moves like it is 1996.
Readable, fair, and exhausting.

Controls

Move WASD camera-relative; Shift creep
Jump Space land and jump again within a quarter second: double, then triple (flip). Hit a pillar mid-air and tap jump to wall-kick.
Crouch / Ground-pound Z hold on the ground; tap in the air to hover-spin and slam
Long jump Z+Space while running. Standing still: backflip. Reverse at speed, then jump: side flip.
Punch, punch, kick Left Click or J. Running: slide kick. In the air: dive.
Grab / Throw Right Click or K. Pick up bombs, or a kneeling boss's tail, and throw or swing.
Camera Mouse click to capture the pointer; Q snaps behind you
Interact E or click at the fog gate. M mute, Esc pause.

The fight

Every attack is telegraphed: a sound, a pose and a mark on the floor. Orange = jump it. Cyan = duck it. A red lane = get out of it. Hits build the boss's stagger; when it kneels, climb its back and ground-pound it, then grab its tail and swing it into the arena bombs.
