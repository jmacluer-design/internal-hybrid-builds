COLOSSUS

CLIMB THE TITAN. TEAR OUT ITS SIGILS.

Dawn over a drowned valley of broken pillars. A titan seventy units tall walks the ruins. You are the climber: swing between the pillars, latch onto the moving giant, cling to its stone hide and find the glowing sigils. Web onto a sigil and yank it loose, or dive onto it from a great height. Three phases, nine sigils, one very large problem.
